# Result approval and official documents

Gradebook entries are working records.
A result snapshot is a saved publication revision.
Families see the latest approved revision.

## Submit and approve results

1. Review the offering's gradebook and learner entries.
2. Submit the calculated result snapshot for approval.
3. A different authorized reviewer checks the revision.
4. The reviewer approves it or rejects it with a reason.
5. Check the published result after approval.

Submission creates a Pending revision.
The submitter cannot approve their own revision.
An identical waiting revision cannot be submitted again.
A reviewer cannot approve an older revision after a newer one exists.
Corrections require a new revision. The prior snapshot remains stored.
A pending correction does not replace the family's approved result.

## Publish a report card

Select the learner and period in the report-card workflow.
The learner and period must belong to the campus.
The period must be Closing, Closed, or Archived.
At least one published subject result is required.
Publish the report card as a saved document revision.
A later publication creates another revision instead of changing the old document.

## Publish a transcript

Open the learner's transcript workflow.
Review the available published subject results.
At least one published result is required.
Publish a saved transcript revision and use its authorized download or print view.

Official documents and generic report exports are separate workflows.
Families can read eligible published documents when the Documents portal area is enabled.
Staff need the relevant report and record permissions.

## Compare results

Enable Ranking to use **Assessment → Rankings**.
Select the relevant cycle, period, and group.
Review the comparison in the context of the selected assessment data.
Keep ranking access limited to the roles allowed by campus policy.
