# Timetables and lesson cover

Use **Teaching → Timetables** after selecting the campus, cycle, and period.
Prepare subjects, course offerings, teachers, and rooms first.

## Build a timetable

1. Create the timetable for the section and period.
2. Add time slots with the required recurrence and date range.
3. Add subject lessons or custom timetable items.
4. Select the teacher and room where required.
5. Run the conflict review.
6. Resolve conflicts and publish the timetable.

A time slot can be a single occurrence or a recurring slot.
Check the recurrence weekdays and date window before publishing.
Custom items represent activities outside the subject catalogue.

Conflict checks cover overlapping slots, teacher conflicts, room conflicts, facility bookings, and rooms out of use.
Teacher checks can include other campuses in the organization.
Review these conflicts before confirming publication.

## Revise a published timetable

Draft timetables are editable.
Published timetables are read-only.
Create the next revision to change a published timetable.
The revision copies the schedule into a new draft.
Publishing the replacement archives the previous timetable for that section and period.
The previous revision remains part of the history.

## Arrange lesson cover

Use the dated substitution workflow for a replacement teacher.
Select the lesson date and eligible replacement.
The application checks staff availability, approved leave, and conflicting teaching work.
Use a dated section override for a section-specific exception.
These exceptions do not rewrite the published repeating schedule.
A closure day can prevent a lesson or cover assignment.

Families see published schedules when the timetable portal area is enabled.
See [facilities](../operations/facilities), [staff availability](../operations/staff), and [calendar events](../operations/calendar).
