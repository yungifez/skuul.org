# Behaviour and safeguarding cases

Enable Discipline and use **Operations → Cases**.
Behaviour and safeguarding records have different access requirements.
Safeguarding permission is required for safeguarding cases.

## Record a case

1. Select the case category.
2. Enter the event time, location, and factual description.
3. Add the involved learners and their participant roles.
4. Add relevant staff or other participants where supported.
5. Save the case and review its access scope.

A future incident time is not accepted.
Record observed facts separately from later decisions.
A normal behaviour permission does not expose safeguarding records.

## Review and finish a case

Add review notes and actions through the case record.
Update its status as the work progresses.
States include Reported, Under review, Referred, Action taken, Resolved, and Closed.
Follow the transitions offered by the page.
A closed case is read-only.
Reopen it through the authorized workflow before adding further notes or actions.

These records do not automatically appear in the family portal.
A campus move does not provide unrestricted access to the source campus's cases.
Use explicit sharing with the applicable restricted-data authority.
