# Parent or guardian

The `parent` role reads eligible records for learners linked to your account.
The school must verify and create each guardian link.
Creating your account alone does not link your children.

## Activate and check your family account

1. Open the latest invitation sent to your own email address.
2. Set your password and complete the required sign-in steps.
3. Sign in. The dashboard shows the current **Notices**.
4. In the sidebar, check the **My school** group. It lists the open pages of one learner.
5. With more than one child, select **Everything of mine**. On the **My school** page, check the names, admission numbers, and campuses of your linked learners.
6. Select a page button under the child whose record you need. The sidebar then follows that child.

The default sidebar shows these entries:

| Group | Entries |
| --- | --- |
| My school, or the child's name when you have several children | The open pages of one child, **Everything of mine** (only with several children), **Notification settings** |
| People | **Students** |
| Operations | **Notices** |

**People → Students** lists only your linked learners.
Use your own account and mailbox. Do not share a learner's password.
Ask the school to [review guardian links](../people/guardians) when a learner is absent or incorrectly linked.
Different campuses can show different portal areas.
Recheck the learner and campus when you move between siblings. The group header shows the name of the child.

## Follow a learner's academic records

| Area | What to review |
| --- | --- |
| **Results** | The subject, term, score, and approval date of each approved result. |
| **Attendance** | Recorded dates and states, including unrecorded entries. |
| **Timetable** | Published lessons and dated schedule changes. |
| **Syllabi** | Published teaching outlines. |
| **Documents** | Published report cards and the issued transcript. |
| **School calendar** | Eligible school events and closure days. |
| **Notices** | Published messages addressed to you. |

The guardian link gives learner access without staff working-campus membership.
Your parent role does not give access to other families' records.
Another learner's portal page shows "Not open to you".
A staff role held on the same account has its own broader authorized scope.
Use [role guidance](./roles#people-with-more-than-one-role) when you are also a teacher or staff member.

A pending result correction leaves the earlier approved result visible.
Approving a new result does not update an already issued report card automatically.
See [result publication instructions](../academics/results) when you ask the school about a revision.

## Review invoices and campus services

Open **Invoices and payments** for the intended learner.
It is a read-only statement with **Still owed**, **Credit held**, and every invoice with its total and balance.
The portal has no family checkout.
Pay through the school's stated process and keep the transaction reference.
Ask the accountant to investigate a mismatch. Give the learner, invoice number, amount, and payment date.

Use **Library**, **Boarding**, **Programmes**, and **Graduation progress** where available for the learner.
Availability depends on the campus feature, portal-area settings, and eligible records.
A missing area does not mean that the record was deleted.
Confidential health, safeguarding, and support records are not family portal areas.

## Submit and follow a request

1. Open **Requests** in the sidebar for the intended learner.
2. Under **Ask the school**, enter **What you need**.
3. Select the **Kind of request**: Document request, Correction request, Appointment request, or Acknowledgement.
4. Enter **More detail (optional)** when the school needs more information.
5. Select **Send**.
6. Under **What you have asked for**, review the request's state and the school's response.

The states are Sent, Being looked at, Answered, Declined, and Withdrawn.
For a correction, identify the record, date or period, current value, and requested change.
For a document, identify the learner, document type, and relevant period.
An appointment request does not reserve a calendar slot automatically.
A document request does not issue a report card automatically.
Use **Take back** to cancel an open request.
See [family request instructions](../family/requests) for the complete workflow.

## Manage notifications and account security

Open **Notification settings** and use **Email me a copy of notices** for each campus.
Account and safety messages, such as a password reset, are always sent.
An email preference changes delivery, not the notice's audience.
Review [profile security](../people/accounts#use-profile-security) and keep recovery codes in a safe place.
Ask the administrator about account status or expired links when recovery fails.

| Problem | Action |
| --- | --- |
| One child is absent | Ask the campus to check that child's guardian link and enrollment eligibility. |
| An unrelated child appears | Report it immediately and ask the school to remove the incorrect link. |
| A published record is absent | Ask whether the area is enabled and the record has been issued or approved. |
| No notice email arrives | Check preferences and ask the school to inspect delivery and publication recipients. |
| A request is Answered but the record remains wrong | Ask whether staff completed the underlying correction. |

Unlinking a learner removes your access and cancels your open requests for that learner.
School records and person accounts remain retained under their normal history rules.
