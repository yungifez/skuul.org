# Family portal

A learner and their guardians read the learner's school records in the portal.
Sign in, then select **Everything of mine** in the sidebar. The page is called **My school**.

A guardian sees each learner that the school linked to them.
A learner sees their own enrollments.
The portal does not use staff permissions. It checks the learner's own account or the guardian link.

## Open a learner's records

![The family overview shows one linked learner at Riverside High School and the areas the family can open.](/images/current/family-overview-light-desktop.webp)

**My school** groups the learners by campus.
Each learner shows their name, admission number, and status, with one button for each open page.

1. Find the learner and check their campus.
2. Select the page, such as **Results** or **Invoices and payments**.

Choose the learner on **My school** before you open a page.
A guardian with two children sees both on **My school**. Each page shows the learner's name and campus at the top.
If a campus closed all its family pages, the learner shows **This campus has closed the family pages.**

## Pages

| Page | What the family sees |
| --- | --- |
| Results | The latest approved result of each course: subject, term, and score. |
| Attendance | Days present, absent, late, and excused, and the attendance rate. |
| Timetable | The newest published week of the learner's class, with only the lessons the learner takes. |
| School calendar | The campus events for families. |
| Notices | Published notices sent to the learner. |
| Invoices and payments | What is still owed, credit held, the invoices, and debts left at an earlier campus. |
| Documents | The latest report card of each term, and the latest transcript. |
| Requests | Send a request to the school, and withdraw it. |
| Library | Loans and reservations. |
| Boarding | The boarding place and nights away. |
| Graduation progress | Progress against the graduation plan. |
| Programmes | The programmes the learner takes part in. |
| Syllabi | The published syllabus of each course, and the topics covered. |

A result that waits for approval is not shown. The family sees the last approved result until the new one is approved.
A rejected result is never shown.
Attendance shows totals only. It counts the days the school took a register.
**Invoices and payments** is read-only. The portal has no online payment. Pay through the school's own process.

## Choose the pages a campus shows

The campus admin turns the portal and each page on or off.

1. Open **School settings** in the sidebar, then **School tools**.
2. Turn on **Student and guardian portal**.
3. Under **Family pages**, turn each page on or off.

A page is shown until you turn it off.
Turning the portal off hides every page. Turning it on again keeps your page choices.
Some pages also need a campus tool:

| Page | Tool |
| --- | --- |
| Attendance | Attendance |
| School calendar | Events |
| Library | Library |
| Boarding | Boarding |
| Graduation progress | Graduation plans |
| Programmes | Programmes |

When the tool is off, the switch shows **Hidden while** and the tool name.

## Who can read what

| Enrollment status | Portal |
| --- | --- |
| Active, Suspended | Shown |
| Withdrawn, Transferred, Graduated | Shown, so the family keeps report cards, transcripts, and any debt |
| Archived | Not shown |

A graduated learner's own account cannot open the portal pages yet. Their guardian can.
Nobody can open the records of a learner they are not linked to.

Use **Notification settings** in the sidebar to choose which notice emails you get.
It shows only when a campus of the learner has Notices open.

## Problems

| Problem | Action |
| --- | --- |
| **Everything of mine** is not in the sidebar | Ask the school to link you to the learner. A guardian with no linked learner has no portal. |
| A learner is missing | Ask the school to check the guardian link and that the enrollment is not archived. |
| A page is missing | Ask the campus to check **Family pages** and the tool the page needs. |
| A result is older than expected | The new result waits for approval. |
| The timetable is empty | The school has not published the class timetable. |
| A document is missing | Ask the school to issue it. |
| You cannot sign in | Use account recovery, or ask the administrator to check your account. |

Use [family requests](./requests) for corrections or questions.
