# Family portal

Sign in with your activated account and open **Everything of mine**.
A guardian sees linked learners. A learner sees their own eligible enrollment.
The campus must enable Portal and the relevant portal area.

## Open a learner's records

![The family overview shows one linked learner at Riverside High School and the areas the family can open.](/images/current/family-overview-light-desktop.webp)

Choose the learner before opening family records.

1. Check the learner name and campus on the overview.
2. Select the required area.
3. Review the selected cycle, period, or date context.
4. Open the available record or authorized document download.

The portal does not require a staff working-campus membership.
It uses the learner's identity or guardian link to check access.
It does not expose other families' records.
Archived enrollments are excluded.
The underlying access service permits several earlier enrollment states, but graduated student accounts have a route limitation.
## Check the result

- Check the learner name and campus on each view.
- Confirm the latest approved result and eligible published documents.
- Verify that unrelated learner records remain inaccessible.

See [known limits](../reference/limitations) for this restriction.

## Available areas

| Area | What you can read or do |
| --- | --- |
| Results | Latest approved result revisions. |
| Attendance | Recorded attendance and summaries. |
| Timetable | Published teaching schedules. |
| Calendar | Eligible campus calendar events. |
| Notices | Published messages addressed to the family. |
| Invoices | Amounts due, held credit, and applicable earlier-campus obligations. |
| Documents | Published report cards and transcripts. |
| Requests | Submit and review requests for the learner. |
| Library | Eligible loans and reservations. |
| Boarding | Eligible boarding placement and nights away. |
| Graduation | Progress against available graduation requirements. |
| Programmes | Eligible programme participation. |
| Syllabi | Published teaching outlines. |

A campus can hide any portal area.
Attendance, Calendar, Library, Boarding, Graduation, and Programmes also require their corresponding feature.
A pending result revision does not replace an approved result.
Draft grades, timetables, and syllabi are not published family records.

The invoice area is read-only. The current application has no family checkout route.
Use [family requests](./requests) for corrections or questions.
Use **Notification settings** to manage notice email preferences.

## Check access as a family

The portal uses the learner's own identity or guardian link, rather than staff campus permission.
The account must still satisfy sign-in rules.
Eligible enrollment states include Active, Suspended, Withdrawn, Transferred, and Graduated; Archived is excluded.
The current route middleware limits graduated student accounts despite the underlying access service's broader allowance.
Test the actual graduate account before promising continued portal access.

1. Accept the latest invitation and complete any required sign-in steps.
2. Open **Everything of mine**.
3. Select the intended learner and verify their campus.
4. Open **Results** to read the latest approved revision.
5. Open **Documents** to read an issued report card or transcript.
6. Open **Invoices** to review charges, payments, and held credit.
7. Use **Requests** to ask the school about an incorrect record.

A guardian with two linked children must choose the intended child before each task.
A pending mark correction does not change the family's approved result.
A newly approved result does not alter an already issued report card.
Ask the school to reissue that document when necessary.
The invoice area provides information; arrange payment through the school's process because family checkout is absent.

| Problem | Action |
| --- | --- |
| No learner appears | Ask staff to verify the guardian link and eligible enrollment. |
| Only one sibling appears | Check the second learner's link and campus portal settings. |
| Area is absent | Ask staff to check the portal area and related feature. |
| Result is older than working marks | Ask whether the correction has completed approval. |
| Document is absent | Ask whether the school has issued that document revision. |
| Account cannot sign in | Use account recovery or ask the administrator to review account state. |
