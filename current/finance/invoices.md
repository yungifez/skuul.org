# Fees and invoices

Use **Finance → Fee invoices**.
The page also links to fees, fee categories, expenses, cash deposits, and financial-period controls.
Select the campus and academic cycle. You need the corresponding finance permissions.

## Prepare fees

1. Create fee categories for the campus.
2. Add fees under their categories.
3. Enter the amounts and applicable details.
4. Review the fee catalogue before raising invoices.

The application uses its configured currency, with NGN as the default.
Set the currency before entering live financial records.
Do not change it as a way to convert existing balances.

1. Select the learner or eligible learner group.
2. Enter the invoice name and due date.
3. Add the required fee lines.
4. Review line amounts, waivers, and fines where applicable.
5. Save the invoice.
6. Open its record and review the amount due.

A fee catalogue amount and an issued invoice line are different records.
Review issued lines when a fee amount changes.
An invoice needs an eligible learner record before a payment can be taken.
Use the print action for an authorized invoice copy.

## Correct an invoice

Unposted invoice structure can be edited where the workflow allows it.
Posted financial records are protected.
Use the account's waiver, write-off, reversal, or credit workflow for posted adjustments.
Do not edit a paid amount directly to simulate money received or refunded.
A waiver reduces the amount owed; it does not record a payment.

Check the financial period if posting is refused.
Closing an academic period does not close its financial period.
For older unposted invoices, an operator can use the reviewed legacy posting command.

## Check the result

- Review the learner, fee lines, due date, and total.
- Check the amount due before recording money.
- Confirm the printed invoice matches the issued record.
