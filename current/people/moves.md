# Campus moves and transfers

Use **People → Campus moves** for moves within an organization.
Use the student transfer workflow when the destination belongs to another organization.
You need the relevant source and destination authority.

## Move within the organization

1. Select the learner and destination campus.
2. Select an eligible destination placement.
3. Preview the move and its effect.
4. Complete the move directly if you hold organization move authority.
5. Otherwise, submit the request for the destination's decision.
6. Review the destination enrollment, placement history, and balance after approval.

Only one pending move is allowed for the learner.
The destination must accept the learner and have capacity.
A duplicate admission number prevents the move.
Approval checks that the source state has not changed since the request.
A same-organization move keeps the enrollment and records placement history.

## Review financial and boarding effects

Balance carry occurs only when the two campuses share a billing group.
The old ledger remains in its campus. Balanced intercampus entries record the carried debt or credit.
A move outside the billing group does not automatically settle or move the balance.
A learner leaving a campus releases their boarding bed.

## Transfer to another organization

A transfer finishes the source enrollment and creates a successor at the destination.
It preserves the relationship between the two records.
It does not copy all private source records or automatically carry ledger balances.
Use explicit record sharing for information the destination needs.

## Check the result

- Confirm the destination placement and dated move history.
- Review old and new campus balances when a billing group applies.
- Confirm that an old boarding bed is no longer occupied by the moved learner.
